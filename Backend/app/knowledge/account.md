# Account: registering, signing in, passwords

## What is KotChomnol (កត់ចំណូល)
KotChomnol is a bookkeeping app for small Khmer shop owners. You record each
sale by speaking it (Khmer or English) or by typing it, review the items, and
save it. The app then shows your revenue for today, this week and this month,
with riel (KHR) and US dollar (USD) totals kept separate. You can export and
share reports and invoices as an image, PDF or Excel table.
It is free to start. A paid Starter plan unlocks more (see the Pro plan article).

## How to register / create an account (ចុះឈ្មោះ)
1. On the home page tap "Start Free" (ចាប់ផ្ដើម) or "Create Account"
   (បង្កើតគណនី). From the Sign In page you can also tap "New here? Create an
   account" (អ្នកថ្មីមែនទេ? បង្កើតគណនី).
2. On the "Create Account" page fill in: First Name (នាមខ្លួន), Last Name
   (នាមត្រកូល), Phone Number (លេខទូរស័ព្ទ, e.g. +855 12 345 678), Email Address
   (អ៊ីមែល), Password (ពាក្យសម្ងាត់) and Confirm Password (បញ្ជាក់ពាក្យសម្ងាត់).
   The password must be at least 8 characters and both must match.
3. Tick the box "I agree to the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy"
   (ខ្ញុំយល់ព្រមនឹង លក្ខខណ្ឌសេវាកម្ម និង គោលការណ៍ឯកជនភាព). You can tap either
   link to read them; they open in a new tab. You cannot register without
   ticking this box.
4. Tap "Register" (ចុះឈ្មោះ).
5. The "Verify Email" page (ផ្ទៀងផ្ទាត់អ៊ីមែល) opens with your email already
   filled in. Open your email inbox, copy the verification code, type it in
   "Verification Code" (លេខកូដផ្ទៀងផ្ទាត់) and tap "Verify Email".
6. You are signed in automatically and taken to your Home screen. If instead
   you land on the Sign In page, your account is verified; just sign in with
   your email and password.

## Register with Google (faster)
On the Create Account page, first tick "I agree to the Terms of Service and
Privacy Policy", then tap "Continue with Google" (បន្តជាមួយ Google) under the
Register button and choose your Google account. No password or email code is
needed; you go straight to your Home screen.
Next time, use "Continue with Google" on the Sign In page.

## Terms of Service and Privacy Policy agreement
Creating an account requires agreeing to the Terms of Service (លក្ខខណ្ឌសេវាកម្ម)
and Privacy Policy (គោលការណ៍ឯកជនភាព) by ticking the box on the Create Account
page, for both "Register" and "Continue with Google". If you tap Register
without ticking it you will see "Please agree to the Terms of Service and
Privacy Policy to create an account." Both documents can also be opened any
time from the "Terms" and "Privacy" links at the bottom of the home page.

## Registration problems
- "Please agree to the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy...": tick the
  agreement box above the Register button.
- "This email is already registered": you already have an account. Tap
  "Already have an account? Sign In", or use "Forgot Password?".
- "This phone number is already registered": that phone number belongs to an
  existing account. Sign in with the email of that account.
- "Password must be at least 8 characters" or "Passwords do not match": fix
  the password fields.
- "Enter a valid phone number": use digits, optionally starting with +855.
- "This field is required": every field on the form must be filled in.

## Verification code problems
- No email: wait a minute and check the Spam/Junk and Promotions folders.
  Make sure the email address you typed was correct.
- "Enter a valid verification code" / invalid code: type the code exactly as
  in the newest email.
- Code expired: the Verify Email page has no resend button. Contact the
  KotChomnol team on Telegram (https://t.me/KotChomnol) for a new code, or
  register with "Continue with Google" instead.
- Sign In says "Please verify your email before logging in.": your email
  was never verified. Open the Verify Email page at /verify-email, enter
  your email and the code from your inbox, and tap "Verify Email". If the
  code has expired, contact support on Telegram.

## How to sign in (ចូលគណនី)
On the Sign In page enter your Email Address and Password and tap "Sign In".
You sign in with your email, not your phone number.
If you registered with Google, tap "Continue with Google" instead.
If you see "Your session has expired. Please sign in again." just sign in again.
Your data is saved to your account, so you can sign in on another phone or
browser and see the same sales.

## Forgot password / reset password
1. On the Sign In page tap "Forgot Password?" (ភ្លេចពាក្យសម្ងាត់?) next to the
   Password label.
2. Enter your registered email and tap "Send Verification Code"
   (ផ្ញើលេខកូដផ្ទៀងផ្ទាត់). You will see "A reset link has been sent to your email".
3. Open the email and tap the reset link. The "Set New Password" page opens.
4. Enter your New Password and Confirm New Password, then tap "Reset Password"
   (កំណត់ពាក្យសម្ងាត់ឡើងវិញ).
5. After "Password reset successful!" you are taken to Sign In; sign in with
   the new password.
If it says the link is invalid or expired, request a new reset email.

## Change password while signed in
Go to Profile (ប្រវត្តិរូប) > Change password. Enter your current password,
then the new password twice, and tap Change Password. If you forgot the
current password, use the "Forgot your password?" link on that screen to get a
reset link by email.

## Log out
Go to Profile > Log out (ចេញពីគណនី).

## Delete my account
There is no delete button in the app. To delete your account and data,
message the KotChomnol team on Telegram (https://t.me/KotChomnol) from the
email or phone number you registered with.
