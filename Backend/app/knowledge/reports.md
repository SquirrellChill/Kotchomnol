# Dashboard, reports and exports

## Home screen
The Home tab shows today's revenue, the number of orders and your recent
transactions, plus quick buttons for Manual Entry and Record Sale.

## Dashboard and analytics (ផ្ទាំងគ្រប់គ្រង)
Open the "Dashboard" tab in the bottom bar. Choose a period: Today, This Week,
This Month or All Time. You will see:
- Total in KHR and total in USD (kept separate)
- Products sold and number of transactions
- Change compared with yesterday / last week / last month (▲▼)
- Weekly or monthly revenue trend chart
- Product share chart and Best Selling Products list

## How to export or download a report / invoice (នាំចេញ)
1. Open the "Dashboard" tab (ផ្ទាំងគ្រប់គ្រង) in the bottom bar.
2. Choose the period: Today, This Week, This Month or All Time.
3. Tap "Export" (នាំចេញ) and pick a format:
   - Table (Excel) / តារាង (Excel): a spreadsheet file (.csv) with date,
     product, quantity, unit price, currency and total for every item. It
     opens in Excel or Google Sheets and shows Khmer text correctly.
   - Image (PNG) or Image (JPG): a picture of the invoice/report.
   - PDF / ឯកសារ: an A4 document for printing; long reports get several pages.
4. The file is saved to your phone's Downloads.
At the top of the menu you can choose the invoice language (Khmer or English).
Table (Excel) is greyed out when the selected period has no sales.

## How to share a report / invoice to Telegram (ចែករំលែក)
On the Dashboard tab choose the period, tap "Share" (ចែករំលែក), then choose:
- As image (ជារូបភាព): previews directly in Telegram chats.
- As PDF (ជា PDF): sent as a document, good for printing.
Your phone's share sheet opens so you can pick Telegram, Messenger or another
app. On some phones you may need to tap Share once more. If the phone can't
share files, the file is downloaded instead and you can send it manually.

## Plans and exports
On the Free plan, daily report exports (PNG/PDF) are included; full report
exports for weekly and monthly periods are part of the Starter plan.

## Put my shop name on invoices
Go to Profile > My Account and fill in "Shop Name" (for example "Sokha Coffee
Shop"), then save. It appears at the top of your exported invoices.

## Why is my revenue zero or missing a sale
Revenue only counts sales you confirmed and saved. Check the selected period
(Today / This Week / This Month), and look in Sales Records to see whether
the sale was saved. Riel and dollar totals are shown separately, so a riel
sale won't appear in the USD total.
