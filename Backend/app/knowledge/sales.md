# Recording, editing and deleting sales

## How to record a sale by voice (ថតការលក់)
1. Tap the round "Add Sale" (បន្ថែមការលក់) microphone button in the middle of the
   bottom bar, or "Record Sale" on the Home screen.
2. Allow microphone access when your phone asks. Without it you can't record.
3. Tap the microphone and say the sale naturally in Khmer or English, for
   example: "កាហ្វេទឹកកក 2 កែវ មួយកែវ 5000 រៀល" or
   "two iced coffee, 5000 riel each, and one bread 1 dollar".
   You can say several products in one recording.
4. You can pause, resume or stop the recording, then tap "Send recording".
5. The app transcribes your voice and extracts each item: product, quantity,
   unit, price and currency.
6. Check the review screen ("Review & Confirm Sale"), fix anything wrong, then
   tap "Confirm & Save Sale". Nothing is saved until you confirm.

## Follow-up questions after recording
If something is missing (for example you didn't say the price or quantity),
the app asks a short follow-up question. Answer by typing, or tap "Answer by
voice". You can also choose "Correct another field", "Add another item" or
"Finish and review". After a few unanswered questions the app sends you to the
review screen to fill in the rest by hand.

## Tips for better voice results
- Say product, quantity and price for every item.
- Say the currency if the price is unclear. If you don't, the app guesses:
  1000 or more is treated as riel, 99 or less as dollars, and in between it
  asks you.
- Record in a quiet place, close to the phone.
- Product names you have sold before are recognized better over time.
- If the result is wrong, tap "Re-record" or edit the items before saving.

## How to add a sale manually (បញ្ចូលដោយដៃ)
On the Home screen tap "Manual Entry" (add a sale by typing items yourself).
Type the product name (the app suggests products you have sold before),
quantity, unit price and currency (KHR or USD), add the item, add more items
if needed, then save. Each item needs a product name, a quantity greater than
zero and a price of zero or more.

## Riel and dollars (KHR / USD)
Each item keeps its own currency. Totals in riel and in dollars are shown
separately, never mixed. Where the app shows an equivalent amount it uses an
exchange rate of 1 USD = 4050 riel.

## Record a sale for yesterday or another date
Use Manual Entry: the first field of the form is "Date" (កាលបរិច្ឆេទ). Pick
the day the sale happened, then add the items and save. The sale is counted
in that day's revenue.

## Product list and prices
There is no separate product list to set up. Just record sales; the app
remembers the products you have sold and suggests them as you type in Manual
Entry, and recognizes them better in voice recordings. Prices are entered
with each sale.

## Do I need internet
Yes. Recording by voice, saving sales and loading your dashboard all need an
internet connection (mobile data or Wi-Fi). If you lose connection, wait
until you are back online and save again, then check that the sale appears
in Sales Records.

## Features not available yet
These are not in the app at the moment: recording expenses or costs,
tracking customers who owe money (credit / pay later), sharing one shop
between several staff accounts, and working fully offline. You can sign in
with the same account on more than one phone. To suggest a feature, message
the team on Telegram: https://t.me/KotChomnol

## How to view, edit or delete a sale
Open the "Sales Records" tab (កំណត់ត្រាការលក់) in the bottom bar, or tap a sale
under "Recent transactions" on Home. Open the sale to see its details, then
tap "Edit" (កែ) to change items, quantities or prices, or "Delete" (លុប) to
remove it. Deleted sales are no longer counted in your revenue.
