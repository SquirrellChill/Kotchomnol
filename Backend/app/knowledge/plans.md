# Plans, price and paying for Pro

## Free plan vs Starter plan (price)
Free Tier, $0: basic sales recording, manual entry, limited voice
transactions, daily transaction history, basic revenue overview, daily report
exports (PNG/PDF).

Starter Plan, $2.99 per month: everything in Free, plus unlimited transactions
and history (weekly, monthly), voice-to-transaction, revenue tracking,
best-selling products, product management and full report exports (PNG/PDF).

## How to upgrade to Pro / Starter (ក្លាយជា Pro)
1. Go to Profile (ប្រវត្តិរូប) > "Go Pro".
2. Select the Starter Plan and tap "Continue — $2.99 / month".
3. A Bakong KHQR code appears. Scan it with Bakong or any Cambodian banking app
   that supports KHQR (ABA, ACLEDA, Wing and others), or tap "Open in Bakong
   app" on your phone.
4. Pay the exact amount shown. The app checks the payment with Bakong
   automatically, so just wait on the screen; you'll see "Account upgraded!".

## Payment details
- The QR code is valid for a few minutes (about 5). If it expires, close it and
  tap Continue again to get a new one.
- One payment gives 30 days of Pro. There is no auto-renewal and no card is
  stored, so nothing is charged automatically. Pay again to extend.
- You can stop any time by simply not renewing.
- If you paid but the account didn't upgrade, keep the bank receipt and contact
  KotChomnol support on Telegram with the transaction details.
