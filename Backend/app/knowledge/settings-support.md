# Profile, settings, privacy and support

## Edit my profile
Go to Profile > My Account (គណនីខ្ញុំ). You can change your first and last
name, phone number, shop name and profile picture (upload a photo or choose an
avatar), then tap "Save Changes".

## Change language Khmer / English
Go to Profile > Language (ភាសា) to switch between English (EN) and Khmer (KH).
On the public pages use the EN/KH button at the top.

## Dark mode
Go to Profile > Theme (រូបរាង) to switch between light mode and dark mode.

## Privacy and my data
Your sales are stored in your own account and only you can see them when
signed in. KotChomnol collects account details (name, email), business and
sales records, and voice recordings only to extract sale details. AI can make
mistakes, so always review extracted items before saving. Payments are
processed by Bakong; KotChomnol never asks for your card or bank password.
KotChomnol does not sell personal information. Full details are on the Privacy
Policy and Terms of Service pages.

## The app is not working
- "Can't reach the database / login service right now": check your internet
  connection and try again in a moment.
- Microphone not working: allow microphone permission for Telegram or your
  browser in your phone settings, then reopen the app.
- Something looks wrong after an update: close and reopen the mini app.
